Meni recording booth

Record every lesson in your own voice. Warm, unhurried, like reading to a friend.

How it works: pick a language, tap Record on a line, read it, tap Stop, listen back, re-record if you like. Each saved take downloads as a correctly named file. Put all files into audio/en/ or audio/es/ in the project, run node tools/build-audio-manifest.mjs, bump the cache version in sw.js, and the app speaks with your voice. Quiet room, phone or laptop mic 20 cm away.
